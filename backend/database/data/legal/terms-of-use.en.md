## 3. Description of the service

### 3.1 Public content

Visitors can, without registering, read about the platform, the assessment paths, the excellence model and its criteria, the published performance indicators, use the "Contact us" form, and use the certificate verification page (/verify).

### 3.2 Self assessment

- A free service in which the entity scores itself against the main criteria and sub-criteria; the result appears as soon as it is submitted.
- The self assessment is an indicative measurement only: it requires no evidence, is not reviewed by an assessor, yields no certificate and is not used as a reference for any other service.
- One open draft is allowed at a time, and previous results are kept in the assessment history.

### 3.3 Accredited assessment

- A paid service; its request opens after the payment is confirmed as complete.
- The entity writes a descriptive text and uploads evidence for each sub-criterion, then submits the request.
- The request is assigned to an assessor who reviews the texts and evidence and sets the scores and notes.
- The assessor may ask for specific items to be completed within a deadline the assessor sets, and decides when asking what happens if the deadline passes without completion: either the assessment continues with what is available, or the request is declined.
- After completion the entity receives the result and the feedback report, and a certificate may be issued under section 11.
- One open accredited assessment request is allowed per entity at a time.

### 3.4 Upcoming services

The advanced assessment (the Award), the Quick Excellence Kit and the leadership path are not available at present. Any content about them that the platform marks as "coming soon" is neither an offer nor a commitment; they will have their own terms and fees when launched.

### 3.5 Development and availability of the service

- We may develop or modify the services, and we will inform you of material changes that affect your open requests.
- We aim to keep the platform available at all times, and we may suspend it temporarily for maintenance, updates or security reasons.

## 11. Certificates

- An electronic certificate may be issued to the entity when the accredited assessment is completed, according to the issue settings adopted by the platform.
- The certificate level is set by the level thresholds published on the platform at the time of issue. If the result is below the minimum threshold, a participation certificate without a level is issued.
- The certificate is issued without the signature of a named person and carries a unique number, a verification code and a QR code.
- Certificate validity period: [[certificate validity period]].
- Suspension: if the assessment is reopened for review, the certificate shows "under review" until the assessment is completed; a replacement certificate is then issued and the previous one shows "superseded".
- Revocation: the platform administration may revoke a certificate for a documented reason, such as data or evidence proving to be incorrect. The reason is communicated to the entity, and the verification page shows the certificate as "revoked" without stating the reason.
- Re-issue: the platform administration may re-issue a certificate for a documented reason, and the previous one shows "superseded".
- Your entity may display the certificate and share its verification code, without altering it, and without using it after it is revoked or while it is suspended.
- Anyone who has the verification code sees on the verification page (/verify): the certificate number and type, the level, the percentage, the entity's name, the issue date and the status.
