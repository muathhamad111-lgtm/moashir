## 1. Introduction and scope

This policy explains how Moashir (the Charitable Work Excellence Model Platform) collects personal data, how it uses, keeps, shares and destroys that data, and which rights you have and how to exercise them.

This policy applies to:

- visitors of the platform;
- entities registered on the platform (civil associations, civil foundations and waqfs) and the authorised person who uses the entity's account;
- anyone who sends a message through the "Contact us" form;
- anyone who verifies a certificate issued by the platform;
- platform staff and assessors who use work accounts on it.

This policy has been prepared in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law, its Implementing Regulation and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom. We make it available to you before your data is collected, and we ask for your consent to it when your entity registers and when you send the "Contact us" form.

## 3. Data we collect

### 3.1 Entity account and authorised person data

When your entity registers we collect: the authorised person's name, the entity's official e-mail address (the single sign-in name of its account), the mobile number if you enter one, the password, the preferred display language and theme, and the two-factor authentication settings if you enable them. The password is stored as a one-way hash that nobody can read, and the mobile number is stored encrypted.

### 3.2 Entity data

The entity's name in Arabic and English, its type, development sector, size, founding year and founding date, region and city, governance grade, number of employees, annual number of beneficiaries, whether it has a strategic plan, licence number, website and logo. This is data about the entity and is not, as such, personal data, but we protect it to the same level.

### 3.3 Assessment data

- In the self assessment: the scores the entity gives itself and its result.
- In the accredited assessment: the descriptive texts you write for each sub-criterion, the evidence and files you upload, the assessor's scores and notes, the result, the feedback report and the certificate.

Evidence may contain personal data of other individuals, such as staff names or attendance lists. The terms of use therefore require the entity to upload only what the assessment needs.

### 3.4 Payment and receipt data

The payment reference, the service paid for, the amount before VAT, the VAT rate and amount, the total, the payment method, the last four digits of the card, the transaction identifier at the payment provider, the payment status and its dates, the entity's legal name at the time of payment, and the payment receipts. When a refund is recorded: the bank-transfer reference, its date, the reason for the refund and the proof of transfer.

Card data is not entered on the platform, does not pass through its servers and is not stored on them; it is entered directly in the payment form provided by the payment provider.

### 3.5 "Contact us" messages

Name, e-mail address, mobile number (optional, stored encrypted), entity name (optional), enquiry type, the message text and later replies, the time of your consent to this policy and its version number, and the language of the correspondence.

If you send the message while signed in, it is linked to your account and appears under "My messages", and the name, e-mail address and entity name are taken from the account.

### 3.6 Consent records

The version of the terms of use and the version of the privacy policy you accepted, the time of acceptance, the IP address and the browser type. Also your consent to marketing messages or its withdrawal, and when that happened.

### 3.7 Security and technical records

- IP address, browser and device type, sign-in and sign-out times, failed sign-in attempts, verification-code events and active sessions.
- An audit log of sensitive operations: who performed them, when, from which address and what changed, without passwords or secrets.
- Web server logs: address, time, requested path without query parameters, and browser type.
- Technical error reports, after personal data has been removed from them.

### 3.8 Certificate verification log

When a certificate is verified we keep the verification result, its time and an irreversible encrypted value derived from the IP address; we do not keep the address itself.

### 3.9 Notifications, change records and requests

In-platform notifications, those sent by e-mail or text message, and your preferences for them. Also the history of changes to the entity profile, requests to change the official e-mail address (old and new address), and the privacy requests you submit.

### 3.10 Platform staff and assessor data

Name, e-mail address, mobile number (optional), roles and permissions, the paths an assessor is qualified for, two-factor authentication settings (mandatory for them), and the actions they perform as recorded in the audit log.

## 4. Sources of data

- Directly from you when you register, complete the entity profile, send requests and messages, and upload files.
- From the entity's authorised person, for the entity's data and for any personal data of other individuals contained in evidence.
- Automatically from your devices when you use the platform: security and technical records and strictly necessary cookies.
- From the payment provider: the payment status, the payment method and the last four digits of the card.
- From the System Administrator: the staff and assessor accounts created by invitation.

## 5. Purposes of processing

1. To create and manage the entity's account, verify the e-mail address and sign in.
2. To provide the assessment services: self assessment, accredited assessment, assignment of requests to assessors, and issue of results, feedback reports and certificates.
3. To collect fees, issue payment receipts, keep financial records and record refunds.
4. To answer "Contact us" messages.
5. To send service notifications about the account, requests, payments and certificates.
6. To protect the platform: prevent unauthorised access and fraud, record operations in the audit log, and handle faults.
7. To enable public verification of certificates.
8. To prepare aggregated statistics and indicators that do not identify anyone.
9. To send marketing messages only to those who explicitly agreed to them.
10. To meet legal obligations and respond to requests from competent authorities.

We do not take fully automated decisions about individuals. The accredited assessment result is set by a human assessor against the approved criteria; the self-assessment result is calculated automatically from the scores the entity gives itself, and no decision is based on it.

We do not use your data for a purpose different from those listed above except in the cases the law permits, and after informing you.

## 6. Legal basis for processing

- Performance of the agreement with your entity (the terms of use): account, entity, assessment and payment data, and service notifications.
- Consent: "Contact us" messages, marketing messages, and questions to the AI assistant when it is enabled.
- Legal obligation: keeping financial records under [[legal basis for the retention period of financial records]], and responding to requests from competent authorities.
- Legitimate interest: security records, the audit log and the certificate verification log, after a documented balancing of that interest against your rights, and without processing sensitive data.
- Employment or service contract: data of platform staff and assessors.

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it, nor processing that relies on another legal basis.

## 7. Mandatory and optional data, and the effect of not providing it

- Mandatory for registration: the authorised person's name, the official e-mail address, the password, the entity's Arabic name, type, sector, size, founding year, region, city, whether it has a strategic plan, and acceptance of the terms of use and the privacy policy.
- Needed to complete the entity profile (the platform reminds you): governance grade, number of employees, number of beneficiaries, licence number and founding date.
- Optional: mobile number, the entity's English name, website, logo, and consent to marketing messages (not pre-selected).
- In the "Contact us" form: name, e-mail address, enquiry type, message and consent to this policy are mandatory; mobile number and entity name are optional.

If mandatory data is not provided, the account cannot be created, the service cannot be provided or the message cannot be answered. Not providing optional data does not prevent the service, but some features may not be available, such as receiving a sign-in code by text message without a mobile number, or showing the entity's logo on the certificate without uploading it.

## 8. Sharing data and recipients

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We make it available only to the following, and only to the extent necessary:

- Platform staff, each according to their permissions and only as far as their work requires: the System Administrator, the Assessment Requests Follower, the Finance Officer, the Content Officer, and anyone granted the privacy-requests permission or the inbox permission.
- The assessor assigned to your entity's request, who sees only the entity data, texts and evidence of that request. The assessor's identity is not disclosed to you.
- A limited number of system owners with full permissions; every action they take is recorded in the audit log together with the reason for any exceptional action.

### 8.2 Processors acting on our behalf under contract

- [[technical operator name]]: development, operation and maintenance of the platform until its formal handover.
- [[in-Kingdom hosting provider]]: hosting of servers, databases, files and backups.
- The payment provider Moyasar: receives card data directly from you. We send it the amount, the currency and a description carrying the payment reference and internal identifiers, without the authorised person's name or e-mail address.
- The active text-message provider ([[active SMS provider]]): receives only the mobile number and the message text. Text messages never contain scores, results, amounts or personal data.
- [[e-mail service provider]]: receives the e-mail address and the message content in order to deliver it.
- The AI assistant provider, only when the assistant is enabled (see section 17).

### 8.3 Other parties

- Competent government and judicial authorities, when there is a legal obligation or a lawful request.
- The public, limited to the certificate data shown on the verification page (see section 13).

## 9. Transfer of data outside the Kingdom

The platform, its database, files, backups and error-tracking tool are hosted inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We do not transfer your personal data outside the Kingdom, or disclose it to a party outside it, except in the following cases and under the conditions and safeguards set by the Law and the Transfer Regulation:

- Payment, text-message and e-mail providers: [[processing location of each provider once confirmed in writing, and the safeguards applied if processing takes place outside the Kingdom]].
- The AI assistant, only when it is enabled: the service provider processes the questions outside the Kingdom, after personal data has been removed from them (see section 17).

In every transfer we limit the data to the minimum needed for the purpose.

## 10. Retention and destruction

We keep data for as long as the purpose requires or for the period the law imposes, and then we destroy it or turn it into anonymised data that identifies no one.

| Data category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account and entity data | For the life of the account. On permanent deletion it is deleted or replaced with a pseudonym |
| Assessment data and evidence | For the life of the entity's account |
| Financial records, receipts, refunds and their proofs | 10 years; kept after the account is deleted, separated from the entity's profile |
| "Contact us" messages | 24 months after the conversation is closed; an open conversation is kept until it is closed |
| Audit log and security records | 5 years, then archived encrypted and removed from the live system; the archive is kept [[audit archive retention period]] |
| Certificate verification log | 365 days |
| Issued certificates and their verification data | Permanently, as a verification record; shown as "revoked" if revoked |
| Consent records | For the life of the account, and after its deletion [[retention period of consent records after account deletion]] |
| Temporary verification codes | Deleted 24 hours after they expire |
| Password reset links | 60 minutes |
| Sessions | Until they expire or you sign out |
| Evidence bundles prepared for download | 24 hours |
| Notifications | [[notification retention period]] |
| Application and web server logs | 14 days for application logs, and [[web server log retention period]] for web server logs |
| Encrypted backups | 30 days, then destroyed |
| Registrations whose e-mail address was never verified | [[retention period of unverified registrations]] |
| Platform staff and assessor accounts | [[retention period of staff accounts after the relationship ends]] |
| AI assistant questions (when enabled) | 90 days, after personal data has been removed from them |

When an entity's account is permanently deleted, we delete its profile, sign-in account, sessions, descriptive texts, evidence and uploaded files, self assessments, drafts, notifications and marketing consent. We keep only what the law or the nature of the service requires: financial records during their period, the audit log and security records, consent records, results after anonymisation for statistical purposes, and the verification records of certificates already issued.

## 11. Your rights

1. The right to be informed of the legal basis and purpose of collecting your data, which this policy provides.
2. The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
3. The right to request a copy of your data in a readable, clear and commonly used electronic format.
4. The right to request correction, completion or updating of your data.
5. The right to request destruction of your data when it is no longer needed, subject to the legal retention periods in section 10.
6. The right to withdraw your consent at any time, for processing based on consent.

You can exercise some of these rights directly from your account: viewing and updating the entity's data, viewing "My messages", changing the consent to marketing messages from the entity profile, and viewing and ending active sessions.

## 12. How to exercise your rights

- Submit your request through the "Contact us" form by choosing the enquiry type "Personal data request", or through [[data protection e-mail]].
- If you send the request while signed in, it is linked to your entity's account. If you send it without signing in, we verify your identity by corresponding with the same e-mail address, and we may ask for proof of capacity before acting on the request.
- We record every request and respond within 30 days. This period may be extended by a further 30 days where acting on the request needs disproportionate effort or where requests are numerous, after informing you of the extension and its reasons.
- We may decline a request that is repetitive, clearly unfounded or needs disproportionate effort, and we will tell you the reason.
- When we correct or destroy your data, we inform the parties to which it was disclosed, where possible.
- The entity's official e-mail address is changed by verifying the new address with a code sent to it; the change then takes effect according to the approval procedure adopted on the platform, and the old address is notified.
- The requested copy is provided as a structured, commonly used electronic file.

## 13. Public verification of certificates

Each certificate carries a unique number, a verification code and a QR code leading to the platform's verification page (/verify). Anyone who has the verification code sees:

- the certificate number and type;
- the level and the percentage;
- the entity's name;
- the issue date;
- the certificate status: valid, under review, revoked or superseded.

A certificate that cannot be verified shows only the words "Not valid for verification", with no other detail.

The verification page never shows the assessor's identity, the reason for a revocation or any detail of the evidence. When a certificate is under review its level and percentage are not shown. The entity's name and logo, as they were at the time of issue, appear on the certificate file itself.

## 14. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies or third-party tracking. We use only the following:

| Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Keeps you signed in and protects the session. Protected, encrypted and not readable by scripts on the page | Until the session ends or after inactivity | Strictly necessary |
| XSRF-TOKEN | Protection against cross-site request forgery | Length of the session | Strictly necessary |
| locale | Remembers the display language you chose; contains no personal data | One year | Preference |
| Local storage for the theme | Remembers the light or dark theme on your device | Until changed | Preference |

When you open the payment page, the payment form is loaded from the payment provider's servers, and the data you enter in it is subject to that provider's policy.

## 15. Data security

We apply organisational, administrative and technical measures aligned with the Essential Cybersecurity Controls issued by the National Cybersecurity Authority, including:

- encryption of connections to the platform;
- encryption of databases and files at rest, with additional encryption of sensitive fields such as mobile numbers;
- storage of passwords as one-way hashes, never sent to any external service for checking;
- two-factor authentication, mandatory for platform staff and optional for entities;
- role-based permissions following least privilege and segregation of duties;
- an audit log that cannot be modified or deleted;
- scanning of uploaded files for malicious software before they are made available;
- temporary download links that work only for the person who requested them;
- automatic ending of sessions after a period of inactivity;
- daily encrypted backups;
- removal of personal data from technical error reports.

If a breach of your personal data occurs, we notify the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. We notify you without delay if the breach could harm your data or your rights, explaining what happened, the measures we have taken and our advice to you.

We ask you to help protect the account: keep your sign-in details confidential, enable two-factor authentication, and tell us immediately about any suspicious use.

- Service notifications about your account and requests are not marketing messages.

## 18. Children and persons lacking full legal capacity

We do not knowingly collect data of children or of persons who lack full or partial legal capacity. If evidence contains data concerning them, it must be masked or anonymised before upload. If we learn that such data was collected without a legal basis, we destroy it.
