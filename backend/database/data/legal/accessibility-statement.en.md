# Accessibility Statement — Moashir

Draft for legal review — last updated: [[approval date]]

## 1. Our commitment

We are committed to making Moashir usable by all beneficiaries, including people with disabilities and older people, in Arabic and English, on computers and mobile devices.

## 2. Standard adopted

- We adopt the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), at Level AA.
- The commitment covers the platform interface in Arabic, right to left, and in English, left to right.
- The commitment covers the public pages, the entities' portal and the platform staff screens.

## 3. Accessibility features of the platform

- Full keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator.
- A "Skip to content" link at the top of pages to move directly to the main content.
- Colour contrast between text and background of at least Level AA; the colours the System Administrator may choose for the interface are limited to a palette tested for this contrast.
- Statuses shown with written text next to the colour, never by colour alone.
- Respect for the operating system's "reduce motion" setting, and buttons to pause motion and autoplay on the home page.
- The exact value of animated numbers is available to screen readers.
- Light and dark themes, and a theme that follows the device setting.
- A language switch (العربية / English) on every page, including the sign-in and registration pages; the user stays on the same page and the choice is remembered.
- Labels for elements and fields that screen readers read in both languages.
- Text-direction isolation for numbers, dates, percentages and file names inside Arabic text, so that they are read and displayed in the correct order.
- Western digits (0-9) in all screens and documents.
- A responsive design that works on screens 360 pixels wide and larger, on the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox.

## 6. Third-party content

The payment form on the payment page is provided by the payment provider from its own servers, and we do not control its design. If you have difficulty using it, please tell us so that we can find a suitable alternative with you.

## 7. Documents

The platform issues documents in PDF format — certificates, feedback reports and payment receipts — in Arabic and English. The audit in section 4 covers the accessibility of these documents: [[result of the accessibility evaluation of PDF documents]]. If you need any document in an alternative format, please contact us.

## 8. Technologies relied upon

Accessibility of the platform relies on the following technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and WAI-ARIA.

## 10. Feedback and contact

We welcome your feedback on any difficulty in accessing the platform's content or services:

- Send a message through the "Contact us" form on the home page and choose the enquiry type "Technical support".
- Mention the page or service, a description of the difficulty, and if possible the browser and assistive technology you use.
